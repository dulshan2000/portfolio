## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the high-caliber discipline of professional cinema production, color grading suites, and VFX composite pipelines. It bridges the uncompromising optical precision of an ARRI ALEXA viewfinder HUD with the elevated, artful curation of an A24 studio editorial and the technical density of DaVinci Resolve.

### Creative Personality & Aesthetic Philosophy
- **Cinematic Rigor:** Visual structures are engineered with strict 90-degree geometry, ultra-fine calibrated reticles, and unyielding dark surfaces. It evokes the atmosphere of a soundstage control monitor at 3:00 AM.
- **Architectural Brutalism meets Precision Minimal:** Zero border radii, surgical 1px optical dividers, and high-density telemetry data create a tactile sense of professional production hardware.
- **Atmospheric Depth:** The foundation is deep, anti-reflective obsidian matte, accented by anamorphic tungsten flares and laser-focused status illuminations.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
Designed for elite Directors of Photography, Lead VFX Artists, Colorists, and AI Video Directors who demand professional utility without sacrificing gallery-grade aesthetic prestige. The UI evokes mastery, absolute color fidelity, technical command, and cinematic gravity.

## Colors

The chromatic palette is rooted in light-sealed mastering suites and hardware telemetry:

- **Primary (`#F59E0B` - Anamorphic Amber):** Represents optical warmth, aperture markings, and primary creative actions. Used for key callouts, primary action states, active timeline markers, and focus rings.
- **Secondary (`#06B6D4` - Chroma Laser Cyan):** Represents mathematical calibration, composite nodes, and pipeline metadata. Used for technical tags, active spatial tracking markers, and pipeline statuses.
- **Tertiary (`#EF4444` - Live REC Crimson):** Reserved strictly for critical recording states, hot outputs, error drops, and destructive operations.
- **Neutral Core (`#070709` Obsidian Matte & `#111216` Elevated Surface):** Zero-specular, pure dark surfaces that eliminate visual fatigue and optimize monitor contrast ratios during grading.
- **Hairlines (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`):** Micro-calibrated division lines imitating optical reticles and camera sensor matrices.

## Typography

The typography implements a tripartite structural hierarchy:

1. **Editorial Display (`Syne`):** Sculptural, high-fashion, and expressive. Used for monumental headings, film titles, reel classifications, and hero statements. It injects A24 cinema presence into technical workflows.
2. **Operational Text (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, neutral, and readable. Selected for narrative descriptions, project summaries, and client briefs to guarantee clarity across small viewports.
3. **Telemetry & Metadata (`JetBrains Mono`):** Monospaced, technical, and always rendered in uppercase. Dictates timecodes (`01:48:22:12`), frame rates (`23.976 FPS`), shutter angles (`180.0°`), sensor ISO, LUT configurations, and composite node labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a modular 12-column architectural matrix framed by precision guides:

- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column fluid grid, 1.5rem gutters, 3rem margins. Interfaces can support high-density dockable panels mimicking NLE and composite multi-pane suites.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout, 1rem gutters, 2rem margins. Auxiliary sidebars collapse into technical drawer states.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 1rem margins. Panels stack vertically while HUD metadata strips lock to top and bottom toolbars.

### Spatial Discipline
All spacing strictly conforms to a 4px/8px incremental rhythm. Layout elements rely on razor-thin grid guides rather than heavy dimensional margins to maximize usable monitor real estate.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established strictly through **optical planes and calibrated luminance layering** rather than fuzzy drop shadows:

- **Plane 0 (Canvas Base - `#070709`):** Total obsidian backplate absorbing light and framing primary footage or canvas renders.
- **Plane 1 (Docked Surfaces & Shells - `#111216`):** Dedicated workspace panels, inspectors, and timelines bordered by 1px hairlines (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Plane 2 (Floating Inspect Panels & Modals - `#17181F`):** Overlays with sharp, unblurred ambient rim-lines (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`) and deep, non-diffuse back-shadows (`0 12px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`).
- **HUD Active States:** Elements achieve focus via razor-thin 1px illumination rings in Anamorphic Amber (`#F59E0B`) or Laser Cyan (`#06B6D4`), producing a glowing optoelectronic appearance.

## Shapes

The design system operates with **absolute sharpness (`roundedness: 0`)**. 

- **No rounded corners (0px):** Buttons, inputs, modals, image viewports, and timeline bars are razor-sharp.
- **Hardware Optical Aesthetic:** Corner framing brackets, viewfinder reticles, crosshairs, and 45-degree chamfer accents on telemetry tags directly emulate camera sensor monitors and physical broadcast deck hardware.
- **Linear Precision:** Dividers are strictly 1px thick with no softening, maintaining visual alignment across intricate node graphs and color wheels.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** High-contrast solid Anamorphic Amber background (`#F59E0B`), deep black text (`#070709`), 0px corner radius, bold JetBrains Mono uppercase typography (`0.8125rem`, tracking +0.1em). On hover, shift to pure white (`#FFFFFF`) background with zero easing delay for tactile snap.
- **Secondary / HUD Button:** Dark surface (`#111216`), 1px hairline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`), white text (`#F4F4F5`). Hover state illuminates border to Chroma Cyan (`#06B6D4`).
- **Telemetry Ghost:** Transparent fill, bordered by subtle corner ticks (`+` markers on all four corners), monospaced text.

### Chips & Telemetry Tags
- **Style:** Compact rectangles with 0px radius, padding `0.25rem 0.5rem`, JetBrains Mono uppercase (`0.6875rem`).
- **Variants:**
  - *REC State:* Red outline (`#EF4444`), semi-transparent crimson fill (`rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.1)`), flashing red recording dot.
  - *Colorist / Node Active:* Laser Cyan outline (`#06B6D4`), subtle cyan fill (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.1)`).
  - *Format Meta (ProRes 4444 XQ / RAW):* Neutral elevated surface (`#111216`), hairline border, muted zinc text (`#71717A`).

### Inputs & Scrubbers
- **Text Inputs:** Sharp-edged `#111216` background, 1px hairline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`), JetBrains Mono text. On focus, outline turns to Anamorphic Amber with no fuzzy glow ring.
- **Parameter Scrubbers:** Numerical input fields with horizontal drag support, displaying value alongside measurement units (`dB`, `K`, `FPS`, `mm`) in muted cyan.

### Lists & Timelines
- Striped or bounded by 1px hairline horizontal dividers (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`). Rows highlight with a subtle `#17181F` background on hover, featuring timecode metadata pinned to the right edge.

### Cards & Viewport Frames
- Sharp 0px panels enveloped in 1px hairline frames. Hero media cards feature ARRI-style optical framing guide corners (white L-brackets at card extremities) and top-bar metadata indicating frame rate, aspect ratio (e.g., `2.39:1`), and scene/take indicators.
